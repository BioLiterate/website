BioLiterate

Privacy policy

Effective August 23, 2026 · BioLiterate, San Francisco, California

BioLiterate is a California 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to building AI literacy in the biomedical community. We keep things simple: we collect only what we need to run our programs and stay in touch with you, and we never sell your information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have — wherever you are in the world.

01

Who we are

BioLiterate (“we,” “us”) is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. We are a nonprofit organization based in San Francisco, California, USA. You can reach us anytime at contact@bio-literate.org.

02

What we collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with BioLiterate:

  • Event registrations. When you register for one of our events (for example, a webinar hosted through LinkedIn Live), we receive the information you provide on the registration form, such as your name, email address, job title, and organization.
  • Contact and newsletter signups. If you reach out through our website or subscribe to updates, we collect your name and email address.
  • Website analytics. Like most websites, ours may collect basic technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) through standard analytics tools to help us understand how people use the site.
03

How we use it — and our legal bases

We use your information to:

  • Send you event access links, reminders, and follow-up materials — to fulfill your registration (performance of a contract)
  • Respond to your questions and requests — our legitimate interest in supporting our community
  • Share BioLiterate news, events, and educational content — with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time
  • Understand our audience and improve our programs — our legitimate interest in running effective educational programming

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

04

Who we work with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to operate our programs, such as LinkedIn (event hosting and registration), our streaming platform, our website host, and email tools. These providers process information only as needed to deliver their services to us, under their own privacy and security commitments.

05

International data transfers

BioLiterate is based in the United States, so if you join us from outside the US, your information will be transferred to and processed in the US. Where required, our service providers rely on recognized safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can contact us for more information about the safeguards that apply.

06

Events and recordings

Some of our events may be recorded for educational purposes. If an event is recorded, attendees are not filmed or audio-recorded; only presenters and panelists appear on the recording. If a live Q&A or chat feature is available, anything you post there may be visible to other attendees and, if the event is recorded, may be included in the recording. We'll let you know at the start of any event whether it is being recorded.

07

Your choices and rights

  • Opt out anytime. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or you can simply reply and ask to be removed.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

We honor these requests for everyone, regardless of where you live. Depending on your location, you may also have specific legal rights:

  • If you're in the EU, UK, or Switzerland: under the GDPR, UK GDPR, and Swiss FADP, you also have the right to restrict or object to our processing, the right to data portability, the right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing already carried out), and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • If you're in California: under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@bio-literate.org. We'll respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally within 30 days).

08

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes described above. As a rule of thumb: event registration lists are used for that event and related follow-up; newsletter contacts are kept until you unsubscribe; and correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle your request. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, and we don't repurpose your data without your consent.

09

Children

Our programs are intended for professionals and adult learners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

10

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: contact@bio-literate.org.
